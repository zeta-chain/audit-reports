# ZetaChain Audit Reports

This folder serves as a comprehensive repository of all public ZetaChain audit reports. At ZetaChain, we believe in transparency, accountability, and the highest standards of security. That’s why we make these audit reports readily accessible to our community, stakeholders, and the general public. By providing access to these documents, we aim to demonstrate our commitment to security and show how seriously we take your trust in our protocol.

## Commitment to Security

Security is at the core of everything we do. We work diligently to identify, address, and resolve bugs as swiftly as possible. Critical vulnerabilities are prioritized and remediated promptly to ensure the continued integrity and protection of our system. For less urgent issues, we take the time necessary to thoroughly analyze and implement long-term, effective solutions. Our goal is to strike the right balance between immediate security needs and sustainable improvements across our codebase.

We also collaborate with leading professional audit firms and independent security researchers before major releases to ensure that our systems are thoroughly vetted before going live.

## Bug Bounty Program

In addition to formal audits, ZetaChain runs an official **bug bounty program** through [HackenProof](https://hackenproof.com/companies/zetachain). We invite ethical hackers and security researchers to test the robustness of our platform. This program helps us identify edge-case vulnerabilities and further harden our protocol by rewarding contributions that help enhance security.

## Competitive Audits

ZetaChain undergoes **competitive audits** for every major blockchain integration. These contests bring in top talent from the security community and allow us to crowdsource rigorous code review in an open, incentivized environment. Past audit competitions include:

- [Code4rena – November 2023](https://code4rena.com/audits/2023-11-zetachain)
- [Cantina – January 2024](https://cantina.xyz/competitions/80a33cf0-ad69-4163-a269-d27756aacb5e)
- [Sherlock – 2025](https://audits.sherlock.xyz/contests/857)

These collaborative efforts with renowned platforms enable us to catch issues early and strengthen our smart contracts and blockchain integrations at scale.
